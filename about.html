Why we started Upside Systems

Before Upside Systems, we built automations, integrations and support tooling for fast-growing software companies: the systems that answer customers at 2 am, route the tricky questions to a person and keep everyone’s data in sync. Meanwhile, the small business owners we knew were spending their Sunday nights on invoices, follow-up emails and spreadsheet updates. Every one of those jobs was something we’d automated a dozen times before.

That’s why Upside Systems exists. We bring the same tooling bigger companies rely on to businesses of 3 to 50 people, at a fixed price, and we stay on to look after it. Our favourite clients are owner-led teams in trades, clinics, agencies and online retail: busy, practical people who would rather spend their time on customers than on admin.

How we work

Measure before we build

Every project starts with your real numbers: hours, costs and what each task is worth. If automation won’t pay off, we say so.

Highest return first

We start with the tasks that free up the most time for the least cost, so you see the benefit in the first month.

People stay in charge

When an automation isn’t sure about a decision, it hands it to a person on your team instead of guessing.

We look after it

Apps change and things break. We monitor every automation and fix problems, usually before you notice them.

What we build with

n8n for workflows, Claude for writing and reading documents, and Jev for fast, reliable decisions, all connected to the tools your team already uses.
