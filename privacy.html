Privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Who we are

Upside Systems LLC (“Upside Systems”, “we”, “us”) builds and maintains business automations for small businesses. We are responsible for the personal information described here. If you have any questions about this policy or your information, contact us at consultation@upsidesys.com.

What we collect

When you use our free audit, we collect the answers you enter: hours spent on tasks, your hourly cost, yearly revenue, team size and expected growth. When you use the package builder, we collect the features and support level you pick. If you send either to us, we also collect your name and email, and optionally your business name, phone number and message.

If you allow analytics, we also collect how the site is used, as described under Cookies and analytics.

How we use it, and why we’re allowed to

We use the details you send us to reply to you, prepare for and follow up on your consultation, and price the package you asked about. We do this because you asked us to (taking steps before a contract) and because we have a legitimate interest in answering business enquiries. We use analytics only with your consent, to understand which pages are useful and improve the site.

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted services to run our business: FormSubmit (which delivers audit and package requests to our inbox), n8n (workflow automation), Google Workspace (email and documents), Google Analytics (site usage, only if allowed), HubSpot (customer records) and Cal.com (scheduling calls). They process your information on our behalf and only for the purposes above. Some of them are based in the United States; where we transfer information from the EEA, UK or Switzerland, we rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) or standard contractual clauses. We may also share information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Your calculator answers and package picks stay in your browser unless you send them to us. If you contact us and don’t become a client, we delete your details and answers after 12 months. For clients, we keep records for as long as we work together, and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Google Analytics data is kept for no more than 14 months.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google LLC, to count visits and see which pages are used. It sets first-party cookies named _ga and _ga_LYQRHSPG2B, which last up to two years, and records pages viewed, the site that referred you, your approximate location (city level), and your device and browser type. Google Analytics 4 doesn’t log or store IP addresses. The site switches off Google signals, ad personalization and advertising cookies, and we don’t combine analytics with anything that identifies you.

Analytics is off unless you choose “Accept analytics”. Until you do, Google Analytics isn’t loaded at all, no analytics cookies are set and nothing is sent to Google. You can change your choice at any time with , also in the footer of every page. Declining also deletes the analytics cookies already set. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a request to opt out and analytics stays off.

The site also uses your browser’s local storage, not cookies, to remember your cookie choice, your audit answers and your package picks on this device. That information isn’t sent to us unless you submit it. Fonts and all other files are served from our own site. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, for security and troubleshooting.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or get a copy of the information we hold about you, object to or restrict how we use it, withdraw consent at any time, or ask us to stop contacting you. Email consultation@upsidesys.com and we’ll reply within 30 days. We won’t treat you differently for using these rights.

If you’re in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. If you’re a California resident, you have the rights above under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA, including the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do neither) and to limit use of sensitive personal information (we don’t collect any). You can use an authorized agent to make a request; we may need to verify your identity first.

Children

This site is for businesses and isn’t directed at children. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Client data

When we build automations for a client, their data stays in the tools they already use. We access only what each automation needs, under a written agreement, and clients can remove our access at any time.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we’ll tell clients by email.
