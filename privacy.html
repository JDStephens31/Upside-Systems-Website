Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Who we are

Upside Systems LLC (“Upside Systems”, “we”, “us”) builds and maintains business automations for small businesses. If you have any questions about this policy or your information, contact us at hello@upsidesystems.com.

What we collect

When you use our free audit, we collect the answers you enter: hours spent on tasks, your hourly cost, yearly revenue, team size and expected growth. If you book a consultation, we also collect your name, email, and optionally your business name, phone number and message.

How we use it

We use this information to prepare for and follow up on your consultation, and to send you your results. We don’t sell your information.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted services to run our business: n8n (workflow automation, where booking requests are received), Google Workspace (email and documents), HubSpot (customer records) and Cal.com (scheduling calls). They process your information on our behalf and only for the purposes above. We may also share information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Your calculator answers stay in your browser unless you send them to us. If you book a consultation and don’t become a client, we delete your details and audit answers after 12 months. For clients, we keep records for as long as we work together, and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you, by emailing hello@upsidesystems.com. We reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses no tracking cookies and no advertising pixels. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser shares your IP address with Google when a page loads. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, for security and troubleshooting.

Client data

When we build automations for a client, their data stays in the tools they already use. We access only what each automation needs, under a written agreement, and clients can remove our access at any time.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we’ll tell clients by email.
