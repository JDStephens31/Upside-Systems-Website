Common questions

What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting, 24/7 monitoring, fixes when an app you use changes, your monthly results report and your change time.

Do I need to buy new software?

Usually not. We connect the tools you already use. Text messages and AI usage have small costs, which we pass on at cost.

Can I start small and upgrade later?

Yes. Start on Starter and move up any time. You only pay the difference in setup fees.

Can I combine packages?

Yes. Upside Complete bundles all three Growth tiers for $2,000 less on setup and $200 less a month.

How long does setup take?

Starter tiers usually go live within two to three weeks of your workflow map call. Growth takes three to five weeks and Scale four to eight, launched in stages so the highest-value automations start working first.

Can I cancel?

Yes. After your first three months, the monthly fee is month to month and you can cancel with 30 days’ notice. The automations live in your accounts, so if you leave we hand over everything we built, with written documentation.
