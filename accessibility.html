Accessibility statement

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Our commitment

Upside Systems wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, voice control or other assistive technology.

The standard we follow

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. This is the standard referenced by the Americans with Disabilities Act guidance, Section 508, the European Accessibility Act (through EN 301 549) and the UK’s accessibility regulations.

What we’ve done

  • Every page works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a “Skip to content” link.
  • Pages use proper headings, landmarks and labels so screen readers can move around them.
  • Text and controls meet WCAG contrast ratios, and pages reflow to a 320-pixel-wide screen without sideways scrolling.
  • Animations are switched off when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Forms explain errors in words, connect them to the field and move focus to the first problem.
  • Results in the free audit are written out in text as well as shown in charts.
  • We test with automated checks (axe-core) and by hand, using keyboard-only navigation.

Known limitations

We’re not aware of any parts of the site that fail WCAG 2.2 AA. If you find one, please tell us.

Report a problem or ask for another format

If anything on this site is hard to use, or you’d like information in another format, email consultation@upsidesys.com with the page and what happened. We aim to reply within two business days and to fix problems within 30 days. You can also ask us to run the free audit with you over the phone or by email instead.
