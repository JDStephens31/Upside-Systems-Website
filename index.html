Before you ask

Does my team need to learn new software?

No. Your team keeps working in the apps they already know. The automations run in the background, and we train everyone on anything that changes for them.

What happens when an automation isn’t sure?

It stops and asks a person. Anything the system isn’t confident about, like an unusual request or an upset customer, goes to someone on your team instead of getting a guessed answer.

What if something breaks?

We’re alerted the moment a step fails, usually before you’d notice. Fixes are covered by your monthly fee, including when an app you use changes how it works.

Is my data safe?

Your data stays in the tools you already use. We connect only what each automation needs, and you can remove our access at any time.

How soon will something be live?

Usually two to three weeks from your workflow map call to your first automation going live. Bigger Scale projects take four to eight weeks, and we launch in stages so you see results along the way.

What’s your busywork bill?

Less than 5 minutes, no sign-up, and you see the numbers before we ever talk.
